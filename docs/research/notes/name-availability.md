# Name Availability: Bee-Themed Planner App Candidates (Waggle, Cella, Melli, Tobee, Hexday, Combly; context: Hive, Hiver)

Research date: 15 November 2026. Method: web search, direct DNS/HTTPS checks of candidate domains (run from this machine on 2026-11-15), GitHub REST API (users and repository search), Apple iTunes Search API (GB storefront). Trademark databases (USPTO/EUIPO/UK IPO) could not be queried directly. The Justia trademark mirror returned HTTP 403. This is not legal advice: a proper clearance search by a trademark professional in Nice classes 9 and 42 (and probably 35 and 41) is needed before public launch.

Domain-check caveat: "does not resolve" (NXDOMAIN or no A record) suggests a domain may be unregistered, but it is not proof. Registered-but-unused domains often have no DNS. Several .app/.dev/.io/.ai domains returned HTTP 200 with an **empty body** from AWS Global Accelerator IPs (76.223.54.146, 13.248.169.48, 3.33.130.190). That pattern fits parked or registrar-hosted pages, but I could not confirm whether they are for sale.

## Is there an existing productivity, planner, to-do, calendar or developer-tool product with each name?

### Takeaway
**Waggle** and **Combly** both already have active or launching products in productivity or calendar territory, so they are high risk. **Cella** has a live Apple productivity app plus a known open-source developer template, so it is medium-high. **Tobee** is medium: there is a bee-themed community app, and "TOBE" focus apps sound the same. **Melli** is medium: no planner found, but melli.app shows "Coming Soon" and the name is strongly tied to a sanctioned bank. **Hexday** is the cleanest by far: no product found, and most domains appear unclaimed. **Hive** and **Hiver** are confirmed established work-software brands, which crowds the bee/hive naming space overall.

### Inferences
- Waggle has an established AI meeting and task tool in the same broad category, plus prominent developer-tool use on GitHub. A bee-themed planner called "Waggle" would very likely face confusion and possible trademark objection.
- Combly has at least three separate live or launching products in 2026, two with calendar or task features. It is also one letter away from Cambly, which hurts search visibility.
- Cella's productivity app is on the Apple platforms a planner would target, and the cellajs developer template is a known name among web developers. Both matter if the app is aimed at developers.
- Hexday is the most distinctive candidate. Note that "hex" names are common among daily puzzle games (Hexly, HexPath), so a planner could get mixed in with games in App Store search.
- Because Hive and Hiver are established, bee/hive productivity names will get more scrutiny. Names that are clearly distinct from "Hive" (such as Hexday) carry less risk.

## Are there prominent companies or brands in other categories with these names?

### Takeaway
**Melli** is the most problematic name outside software: Bank Melli Iran is under heavy US, EU and UK sanctions. **Waggle** is used by a golf guide and pet and golf apps, and **Tobee** by an industrial pump maker and an Italian digital agency. **Cella** is a common word and personal name. **Hexday** has no notable use elsewhere.

### Cited Findings
- **Melli:** Bank Melli Iran is on the OFAC SDN list (designated 5 Nov 2018, tied to the IRGC-Qods Force). It is also sanctioned by the EU and UK (asset freeze) and was the subject of a US State Department action in August 2026. — [OFAC](https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov/Details.aspx?id=25578); [Iran Watch](https://www.iranwatch.org/library/governments/united-states/executive-branch/department-state/us-sanctions-sever-financial-lifelines-iranian-regime); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/cbuae-sanctions-bank-melli-iran-financial-transactions-ban). On GitHub, "Melli" also appears in Iran-related tooling, for example AghaFarokh/CF-Scan-TolidMelli (143 stars). — [GitHub API search](https://api.github.com/search/repositories?q=melli+in:name&sort=stars)
- **Waggle:** waggle.com is the "Washington Area Golfer's Guide", running since 1999. — [waggle.com](https://waggle.com). The GB App Store also has "Waggle Pet App" (Nimble Wireless) and "Waggle" (Waggle Golf LLC). — [iTunes Search API](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=waggle&entity=software&country=gb)
- **Tobee:** tobee.com belongs to Tobee Group, a Chinese maker of slurry pumps. — [tobee.com](https://tobee.com). tobee.co is "to bee", an Italian digital company in Sant'Anastasia (NA) with several business units. — [tobee.co](https://tobee.co). ### Inferences
- Linking a consumer brand to a sanctioned bank, even by coincidence, is a reputation and search-results problem. Searches for "Melli app" in some regions may bring up banking apps.
- Uses of "Waggle" and "Tobee" in other categories (golf, pets, pumps) carry little trademark risk on their own. Waggle's risk comes from the productivity products described above.

### Inferences
- **Waggle:** If kept, it needs a qualifier, for example "Waggle Planner" (waggleplanner.com appears open) or "Waggleday". Even so, conflict with Waggle AI in productivity remains, so the risk stays medium-high. A clearly distinct name is better.
- **Combly:** Not recommended. Variants like "Comb Planner" or "Honeycomb Day" do not get away from the Combly products, and "Honeycomb" is itself heavily used (for example Honeycomb.io in developer observability; not verified here).
- **Cella:** Possible with a qualifier ("Cella Planner", cellaplanner.com appears open). Expect App Store confusion with the existing Cella productivity app.
- **Melli:** "Melli Planner" would need checking. The bank association and the "Coming Soon" page at melli.app are hard to avoid.
- **Tobee:** "Tobee Planner" or "gettobee" (gettobee.com appears open) are possible, and the GitHub handle is free. Confusion with TOBE focus apps and the toBee community app remains.
- **Hexday:** Best overall. Practical options are hexday.app or hexday.dev, both apparently open. Consider buying hexday.com if it is for sale (its owner is unknown).

### Gaps
- I did not check whether hexday.com or the empty-page Waggle domains are listed for sale.
- Social handles (X, Instagram, TikTok) were not checked.
- DNS failure does not prove a domain is unregistered. Confirm availability with a registrar before relying on it.
